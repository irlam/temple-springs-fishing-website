01 / THE PLACE

It starts with
a sense of place.

Temple Springs is the focus of a proposed fishing restoration project in Bolton — bringing together care for the natural environment and an interest in responsible angling.

Before plans can become practical work, there are important questions to answer about permissions, the condition of the site and what local people would like to see. We want to take the time to understand the place properly.

This website shares an emerging idea and invites local knowledge. It does not announce an open fishery, an agreed lease or permission to visit.

INITIAL PROJECT NOTE

Planning stage

Opening the conversation

The idea is to explore a community-led fishing restoration project at Temple Springs. The immediate focus is understanding rights and permissions, gathering local perspectives and identifying what needs to be assessed.

There is no confirmed opening date, fishing offer or restoration timetable. Further updates will be added when there is verified progress to share.

PLEASE READ BEFORE PLANNING A VISIT

A proposal, with permissions still to establish.

Angling rights and fishing access at Temple Springs are not currently confirmed. This is not an operating fishery. Nothing on this website grants permission to fish, enter the site, carry out work or organise activities. Please respect landownership and existing access restrictions. Any future activity would depend on the necessary rights, permissions and assessments.
