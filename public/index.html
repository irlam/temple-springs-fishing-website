Welcome to Temple Springs Fisheries — our vision for a welcoming place to fish, unwind and enjoy the outdoors in Bolton.

We’re building a simple day-ticket experience for the future: choose your date, keep your ticket on your phone and enjoy a thoughtfully managed day by the water.

Behind it is a community-led effort to explore bringing angling back to Temple Springs. Rights and access are still being established, so we are not inviting visits or fishing yet.

A fishery in the making.

Temple Springs remains a proposed community-led fishing restoration project. We’re working towards a welcoming, well-managed future for the water, subject to the necessary permissions and assessments.

Real fishing bookings are not open. The demo takes no payment and its tickets grant no permission to enter or fish. We’ll update this website when there is confirmed progress to share.
